## 📖 Abstract

Docon is a web-based digital healthcare platform designed to modernize patient management, telemedicine consultations, and electronic health records (EHR).

The platform enables:

- Online appointment scheduling  
- Secure electronic health record management  
- Telemedicine consultations (chat-based)  
- Digital prescriptions  
- Billing & payment integration  
- Health reminders & notifications  

The project was developed using the MERN stack (MongoDB, Express.js, React.js, Node.js) to deliver a scalable and secure healthcare solution.
